GOVERNMENT HEALTH SCHEME

CGHS Hospital Consultancy

Consultancy and operational guidance related to Central Government Health Scheme hospital processes, documentation and coordination.

The Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS) provides healthcare facilities to central government employees and pensioners. For hospitals empanelled or seeking CGHS empanelment, proper documentation, process management and coordination are essential. Shivam Group of Enterprises helps hospitals navigate CGHS requirements effectively.

Empanelment & Documentation

Guidance on CGHS empanelment documentation requirements, facility standards and the process of maintaining or renewing CGHS hospital empanelment.

Process Guidance & Compliance

Operational guidance on CGHS patient treatment processes, rate schedules, billing procedures and compliance with scheme guidelines and requirements.

Coordination Support

Assistance with coordination between the hospital and CGHS administrative machinery, facilitating clearer communication and smoother processing of CGHS cases.

Operational Assistance

Support in establishing efficient internal processes for managing CGHS beneficiary admissions, treatment tracking and discharge documentation.
